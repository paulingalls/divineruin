Bump: minor

Damage spells now hurt what they hit: outside the Choir, a mage's spells used to report damage that never landed. Hollow troops, the Hollowed Knight and the Veilrender fight by their bestiary rules. Every Tier 1-2 Hollow creature has an encounter the DM can start. The Hollowed Knight gets back up at 1 HP, attacks with two corrupted blades and commands its mawlings. The Veilrender regenerates unless radiant light or a Veil Ward stops it, corrodes everything living that stands in its field, and feeds on any spell that is not radiant.

Release steps: none.
