Bump: patch

A guardian can spend 3 Stamina to take a hit's damage for an ally within 5 ft.
The guardian's own reductions apply, and the struck ally still receives the
hit's non-damage effects. One guardian can Intercept each hit.

Remaining Guardian milestone designs include Shield Mastery's 1d8 reduction
and Warden-Commander's 10-ft range in `docs/game_mechanics/game_mechanics_archetypes.md`.
The Thornridge's Stand entry in `docs/game_mechanics/game_mechanics_crafting.md`
specifies a 15-ft override and once-per-long-rest Last Wall, both pending.
