Milestone 38 shipped: any authenticated player's spoken turn now acts on that player's own state. That covers activation, checks, queries, standing and choices, downtime and repairs. Party movement, quest outcomes and combat end fan out to every member. The DM's hot layer names the speaker in exploration and combat without I/O, and the string actor binding is gone. They also proved guest-scoped check, travel and dispatch handoff from real speech. The capstone's training step is debt `b6784d9c`.

Story close caught four defects that tests passed over: 13 guards that could never red, 31 combat tests an executor never ran, a 670-line test file kept under the cap by module-level `# fmt: off`, and a hot layer that put four DB reads on the 1500 ms path. The last two were marked "noted" by the reviewer. The lead reclassified them as blocking and sent them back to a fresh teammate. A recurring mocked-test error was traced with a connect_tcp spy rather than called a flake. The cause was an unstubbed portrait POST that would have been paid image generation whenever a dev server was up (`9bdf560a`).

**What it missed.** Stories 104-106 landed pyright errors, and story-107 landed a mobile type error that only pre-push would have caught. The commit hook ran ruff and eslint but no typecheck. It now runs pyright and per-workspace tsc (`361da5b4`, `57899d3f`). Executors handed back after running only their Verify, so story tier and typecheck reds surfaced at land and cost rework rounds. The paid capstone was scripted against the wrong agent flow. The first approved run exposed that, and the second showed the DM declining training in fiction.
